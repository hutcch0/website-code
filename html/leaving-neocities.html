Leaving Neocities

Posted on: October 3, 2026

This site doesn't live on Neocities anymore. It now runs on my own VPS at site.hutcch.casa, right next to the rest of my homelab stuff. Same pages, same look, just my own server.

With the move I cleaned house: the old Neocities Supporter badges are gone, a few dead pages got removed, and the old hit counter is replaced with my own. It counts visits to the home page and the number on every page updates once a day at midnight.

The biggest thing I've been building is Yuki, my desktop companion. She has her own PNG-tuber sprites, talks out loud through my speakers, notices what I'm doing on my PC, and I can chat with her from a little corner window. She also lives on the server so the chat stays in sync.

On the Minecraft side I've been working on my own mod and on a tool that uses AI to turn prompts into litematic builds. The CC:Tweaked programs are still on GitHub too.

And there's finally a guestbook! Leave a message and it'll show up once I've approved it. Thanks for sticking around.
